Six years of UX, agency and in-house.

I'm a senior UX designer with five years of agency experience and a further year in-house as part of a global design team. I work across the full process, from early discovery and stakeholder research through to strategy, information architecture, and design handed over ready to build.

Accessibility, sustainable design, and cognitive clarity sit at the centre of how I work. I am drawn to organisations with a real communication challenge, where research meaningfully changes the outcome rather than simply validating it.
